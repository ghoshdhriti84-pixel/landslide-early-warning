    Method & Honest Limitations

    How a prediction is produced

    1. Inputs are validated and combined with the location's static terrain susceptibility.
    2. A separate transparent weighted index produces an independent reference score.
    3. A Random Forest classifier classifies the reading into Low / Moderate / High / Very High.
    4. Per-reading influences are approximated as importance × standardised deviation from the training mean, normalised to 100 %.

    What this is NOT

    • Not a physical or statistical landslide-hazard model (no slope stability, geology or infiltration modelling).
    • Not trained on field landslide records unless you place your own labelled data/dataset.csv in the data folder.
    • No accuracy figure is claimed from synthetic data — any metric shown is computed on this project's own synthetic labels and describes only the reproduction of that rule.
    • Not usable for evacuation or life-safety decisions.
