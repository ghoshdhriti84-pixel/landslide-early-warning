Safety Guidance by Alert Level

Advisory

Stay alert. Avoid steep cut slopes, fresh road cuttings and gully beds during continued rainfall. Keep monitoring local weather updates.

Warning

Prepare to move. Shift to flatter ground if rainfall continues; keep documents, medicines and a torch ready; avoid hill roads and watch for cracks, tilting trees or new springs.

Severe

Act now. Evacuate steep-slope and low-lying settlements, move to a pre-identified safe shelter and follow district administration instructions. Call 112 in an emergency.

These are general precautions used by the prototype. Official evacuation orders come only from government authorities — this prototype is not an emergency-warning service.
